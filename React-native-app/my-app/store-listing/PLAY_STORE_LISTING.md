## Full description (max 4000)
**English**

Worker Search connects people who need work done with verified, skilled workers across Tanzania – plumbers, electricians, cleaners, carpenters, painters, house help and many more.

FOR CLIENTS
• Book a service in minutes: choose the work, say how many workers you need, and when.
• Hire for longer: post a job for house help or staff for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months and pick from the applicants.
• Book a worker directly: see a worker's profile and send them a request.
• Every worker's ID is checked by our team before they can take jobs.
• Chat and call the worker once they accept, follow the job, and rate the work.

FOR WORKERS
• Get jobs near you – bookings, job posts and direct requests from clients.
• Accept or decline, clock in and out, and see what you earn.
• Build your profile: your skills, experience, documents and ratings.
• Get notified the moment a new job arrives.

SAFE AND SIMPLE
• Verified workers only.
• Your address and phone number are shared only after a job is accepted.
• Available in English and Swahili.

Worker Search is made by EasyFix Company Limited, Tanzania.

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Why | Optional? |

| Phone number | Yes | No* | Account, app functionality (worker and client contact after a job is accepted) | Required |
